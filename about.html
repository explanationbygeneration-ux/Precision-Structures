Who We Are

35+ Years of
Engineering Excellence

Precision Structures has been manufacturing engineered truss systems in Hooper, Utah since 1990 — building a reputation for accuracy, reliability, and production capacity across the Rocky Mountain region.

Precision Structures opened its doors in 1990 in Hooper, Utah — and from day one, the focus was on doing the work right. No shortcuts, no compromises on materials, and no missed deadlines.

Where other manufacturers prioritize volume over quality, Precision doubled down on engineering accuracy and production consistency. Every truss that leaves our facility is built to spec, inspected for quality, and delivered on schedule.

“We have the engineering depth and production capacity to handle the most complex projects in the region — and the 35-year track record to back it up.”

Over three decades of steady growth, that founding commitment to precision has never changed. What started as a modest operation has grown into one of the most experienced truss manufacturers in the Rocky Mountain region — serving contractors, builders, and developers across six states.

Our in-house engineering team, purpose-built manufacturing facility, and direct delivery fleet mean we control the entire process from design through installation support. When you work with Precision, you work with the people who actually engineer and build your trusses.

That’s the Precision difference — and after 35+ years, it’s still what sets us apart.
