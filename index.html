Who We Are

Experience.
Precision. Results.

Since 1990, Precision Structures has operated out of Hooper, Utah — manufacturing engineered truss systems for contractors, builders, and developers across the Intermountain West.

“We have the engineering depth and production capacity to handle complex projects — and the track record to prove it.”

Over three decades of steady growth, we’ve built a reputation for accurate engineering, tight tolerances, and on-time delivery. Our in-house design team works directly with your plans to produce truss systems that are ready to install the day they arrive on site.

Whether it’s a standard residential package or a complex commercial build, we deliver what we quote, when we quote it.
